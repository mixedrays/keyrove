What a keydown costs

One keyRove call, timed in a real browser: on pages of up to 20,000 items, with the listener on the group or on the document, and with up to 1,000 focus keys.

    How it is measured

    • Each page is built before timing starts. Building it is not part of any case.
    • keyRove is called directly with a hand-built event whose currentTarget is the group or the document, so event dispatch is left out.
    • Each case warms up, doubles its batch until one batch takes at least 10 ms, then times 15 batches. The tables give the median and the p10–p90 range of the time per call.
    • The cases run in a page of their own, shown in the corner while a run is going. None of this page’s markup is part of what they count or scan.
